DMC IA

DMC IA is an AI automation brand. We build autonomous AI agents, workflow automation systems, and intelligent business operations for SMEs across Portugal and Europe — delivering technology that was previously only accessible to large enterprises, at a scale and price that works for small and medium businesses.

What DMC IA Does

DMC IA specialises in one thing: making AI automation practical and accessible for businesses that don't have enterprise budgets, internal AI teams, or months to spend on implementation. We design, build, and deploy AI systems that automate the repetitive operational tasks that consume disproportionate time in small and medium businesses.

Our approach is direct. We audit your current operations, identify the highest-ROI automation opportunities, build the AI systems that address them, and deploy those systems so they run autonomously — without requiring ongoing technical management from your team. The AI does the work. Your team does the thinking.

DMC IA operates across Portugal, France, Belgium, Switzerland, and Luxembourg, with services delivered in Portuguese, French, and English.

Our Core Principles

Practical over theoretical

Every system we build solves a specific, measurable business problem. We don't propose AI for AI's sake — we deploy it where it delivers clear, quantifiable value.

SME-first design

All DMC IA systems are designed for the operational reality of small and medium businesses — lean teams, constrained budgets, and limited technical capacity.

Measurable results

Every deployment is measured against specific KPIs: time saved, cost reduced, errors eliminated. We build for outcomes, not for complexity.

Security by design

Every AI system we build is built with strict data controls, permission boundaries, and full audit trails. Security is not an add-on — it is a core design requirement.

Brand-only authority

DMC IA operates as a brand, not as individuals. Our authority comes from results, case studies, and technical expertise — not personal profiles or founder stories.

Long-term partnership

We don't build and disappear. DMC IA provides ongoing monitoring, optimisation, and support — ensuring AI systems continue to deliver value as your business evolves.
