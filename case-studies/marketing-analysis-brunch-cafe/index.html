Case Study

AI Marketing for Brunch Café: 3× Customer Engagement

Sector: Restaurants & F&BService: AI Marketing AutomationTimeline: 2 weeks to deploy

The Problem

A popular brunch café with two locations was struggling to maintain consistent marketing activity. The owner handled all marketing personally — creating social media posts, responding to reviews, sending occasional email newsletters, and managing the Google Business Profile — while also managing daily operations. The result was sporadic, inconsistent marketing that didn't reflect the quality of the actual café experience.

Social media posts were published irregularly, sometimes going weeks without updates. Google reviews went unanswered for days. The email list of 1,200 subscribers received a newsletter roughly once every 6–8 weeks. Promotional campaigns for seasonal menus were announced too late to build meaningful anticipation.

The owner needed AI to take over the entire marketing execution layer — creating content, scheduling posts, responding to reviews, and managing email campaigns — without requiring daily attention.

The AI Solution

01

AI Content Generation & Scheduling

An AI agent trained on the café's brand voice, menu, and aesthetic generates social media content (Instagram, Facebook) weekly — 5 posts per week per location — and schedules them at AI-optimised peak engagement times.

02

Automated Review Response

AI monitors Google, TripAdvisor, and Facebook for new reviews. For positive reviews, AI generates and posts personalised thank-you responses within 2 hours. For negative reviews, AI drafts a response for owner approval before posting.

03

Email Campaign Automation

A weekly email newsletter automatically generated from the week's specials, seasonal menu updates, and café news — sent to the subscriber list every Thursday morning. Special promotional campaigns triggered automatically for seasonal events and holidays.

04

Customer Behaviour Analysis

Monthly AI analysis of engagement data, customer visit patterns from booking data, and review sentiment — delivered as a simple summary report with recommended menu or marketing adjustments for the following month.

Results After 60 Days

3×

Increase in average social media engagement rate

45%

Increase in repeat customer visits (tracked via reservation data)

94%

Google review response rate (from 12% before AI deployment)

8h

Hours per week reclaimed by the owner from marketing tasks

Within 60 days, the café's social media presence became consistent, visually coherent, and actively engaging. The email open rate increased from 18% to 31% due to improved content consistency and timing. The owner now spends under 30 minutes per week reviewing AI-generated content before it publishes — versus 8+ hours of manual marketing effort previously.

The most significant business outcome: a 45% increase in repeat customer visits, attributed primarily to the AI-driven email nurture sequence that keeps the café top-of-mind between visits.
