What MIDAS IA does

AI Agent Orchestration

Creates, deploys, and manages autonomous AI agents tailored to each department's specific workflows and tools.

Local Deployment

Runs entirely on-premise within the client's infrastructure. No data sent to external cloud servers — full privacy by design.

Adaptive Domain

Configures to any business domain — finance, operations, HR, customer service — adapting to the client's specific tools and processes.

Task Automation

Automates repetitive and complex tasks across the organisation — from document processing to multi-step operational workflows.

Architecture & Technology

Hermes Agent Framework

MIDAS IA is built on the Hermes Agent architecture — a multi-agent framework designed for structured reasoning, tool use, and autonomous task execution. This foundation enables MIDAS to coordinate multiple specialised agents working in parallel, each operating within a defined scope while sharing a unified context layer.

On-Premise by Design

Unlike cloud-based LLMs, MIDAS IA is deployed directly within the client's local environment. This architecture ensures that sensitive business data never leaves the organisation's infrastructure, enabling compliance with GDPR and sector-specific data protection requirements.

Developed by DMC IA

MIDAS IA was conceived, developed, and is maintained exclusively by DMC IA — an AI automation company specialising in deploying intelligent systems for SMEs across Portugal, France, Belgium, and Switzerland. DMC IA is the sole creator and owner of the MIDAS IA model.
