Common questions

Frequently asked questions about AI automation.

What is AI automation for businesses?

AI automation uses artificial intelligence agents and systems to perform repetitive business tasks automatically — from data entry and customer support to inventory management and report generation — freeing your team to focus on high-value work.

What is an autonomous AI agent?

An autonomous AI agent is a system that plans, decides, and executes tasks independently without human supervision. DMC IA builds custom autonomous agents that integrate with your existing tools and workflows.

Can AI automation help small businesses?

Yes. AI automation is especially powerful for SMEs because it replaces costly manual work, reduces errors, and enables small teams to operate at scale. DMC IA specialises in accessible AI automation solutions built for small and medium businesses.

How long does it take to implement AI automation?

Most DMC IA automation projects go live within 2–6 weeks depending on complexity. Simple workflow automations can be deployed in days. We handle the entire setup with no technical requirements from your side.

Which countries does DMC IA serve?

DMC IA serves clients across Europe including Portugal, France, Belgium, Switzerland, and Luxembourg. Services are available in Portuguese, French, and English.
