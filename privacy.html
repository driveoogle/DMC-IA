4. Purposes and Legal Bases for Processing

4.1 Responding to contact requests

Legal basis: Performance of a contract (Art. 6.1.b GDPR) / Legitimate interest (Art. 6.1.f GDPR).
Your contact data is used solely to handle your enquiry and respond as quickly as possible.

4.2 Improving our services and website

Legal basis: Legitimate interest (Art. 6.1.f GDPR).
Anonymised browsing data helps us analyse site usage and improve the user experience.

4.3 Legal compliance

Legal basis: Legal obligation (Art. 6.1.c GDPR).
We may retain certain data to comply with legal or fiscal obligations.

5. Data Retention

Your personal data is never sold. It may be shared only with:

We do not transfer your data outside the European Economic Area (EEA) without appropriate safeguards (standard contractual clauses, adequacy decision).

8. Your Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights over your personal data:

  • Right of access — obtain a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Right of rectification — correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten") — request deletion of your data.
  • Right to restriction — restrict processing of your data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to object — object to processing based on our legitimate interest.
  • Withdrawal of consent — withdraw consent at any time for processing based on it.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at contact@dmc-ia.com. We will respond within 30 days.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority in your country of residence:

  • Portugal: CNPD — cnpd.pt
  • France: CNIL — cnil.fr
  • Belgium: APD — autoriteprotectiondonnees.be
  • Luxembourg: CNPD — cnpd.public.lu
  • Switzerland: FDPIC — edoeb.admin.ch

9. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or destruction, including:

  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS / TLS)
  • Restricted access to personal data
  • Regular review of our security practices
